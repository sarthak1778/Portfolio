04 // Major Engineering Project

Hybrid ANN-PID PMSM Speed Control

In-depth architectural modeling and adaptive neural speed regulation for Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors under dynamic mechanical load perturbations.

ELECTRICAL DRIVE CONTROL & SIMULATION

Adaptive Closed-Loop Speed Control Architecture

Addresses severe dynamic settling sluggishness and overshoot in conventional fixed-gain PID motor drives. A multi-layer feed-forward neural network continuously evaluates instantaneous speed error $e(t)$ and its derivative $\frac{de}{dt}$ to adjust PID parameter matrices in real time.

System Signal Flow Architecture (Field-Oriented Control Loop)

1. d-q State-Space Modeling

Simulates surface-mounted permanent magnet synchronous dynamics in the rotor synchronous reference frame via Clarke and Park transformations.

2. ANN Adaptive Tuning

Feed-forward backpropagation neural net trained on non-linear operating envelopes to adjust Kp, Ki, and Kd gains without human manual retuning.

3. Inverter & SPWM Stage

Sinusoidal Pulse Width Modulation switching high-frequency IGBT bridges with carrier frequency harmonics filtering.

4. Transient Validation

Comparative benchmark against conventional Ziegler-Nichols PID, demonstrating reduced dynamic settling time under step load torque injections.
